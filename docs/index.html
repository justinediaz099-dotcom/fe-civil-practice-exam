Your exams

Every exam you finish is kept here with its score, area breakdown, and every missed question with its solution.

How this works

110 questions drawn to match the official NCEES FE Civil exam specification, with the same number of questions per knowledge area as the real test. Most are multiple choice; about a quarter are numeric entry, because the real computer-based test mixes both. You get 350 minutes, the same working time as the real exam once the tutorial and scheduled break are removed.

Every question has a full worked solution, the formula it comes from, and—when you pick a wrong option—an explanation of the specific mistake that option represents.
